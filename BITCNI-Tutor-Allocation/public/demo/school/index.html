Tutor Assignment Management — School Portal

Use this portal to manage tutor requests for your School. You can:

  • View the status of ongoing requests for tutors for your School
  • View records of tutors assigned to your School
  • Create a new request for a tutor to be assigned to your School
  • Edit a pre-existing request for a tutor

Browse schools recorded on the system

Search for your School below and select it. Your selection will carry through to the other pages, letting you view, submit, and edit tutor requests on behalf of that School. Use the search fields directly below to filter the displayed Schools



Select your School from the options below to view and change requests for tutors for the selected School

Click 'Select' next to your School to set it as your active School. Your selection will be remembered as you navigate between pages
